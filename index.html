Design system foundations that gave a brand its grammar

Design system · Whirlpool / Brastemp · Senior PD

Context
Brastemp's primitive and semantic tokens existed in the new Orbit design system, but with no underlying visual logic or rules to govern how they should be applied.
Challenge
Build technical rigor, contrast, and traceability into the brand without killing the personality that makes it recognizable, and without locking other designers in.
My contribution
I built the foundations single-handedly in 3 weeks, typography, color, border, pattern, and motion, plus the cross-application rules and the branded-moment criterion the brand never had. My hardest call, resolving the AS IS vs. IDEAL tension without escalating.
