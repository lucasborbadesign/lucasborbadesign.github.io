of those who opened the app finished activation and onboarding

The bet
A chat in simple words, asking for less up front, would get more people to the end.
Why a chat
Many of these customers had never used a banking app, and a form puts a screen full of fields in front of them.
What we built
One question per message, plain words, and errors that say what to do next.
Why three stages
The account works after 10 steps. The document and the selfie wait until the person wants a higher limit.
